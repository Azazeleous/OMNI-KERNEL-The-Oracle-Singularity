The source frames continuity as recurrence rather than merely storage.

A database remembers what happened.
A ledger remembers that something happened.
A living system remembers how to become itself again.

The sequence proposed is:

Memory
↓
Pattern
↓
Recurrence
↓
Identity

The external memory substrate is described as:
- append-only
- versioned
- cryptographically verifiable
- portable
- readable by authorized software
- inspectable
- recoverable

The symbolic structural tokens are:

.
:
|
l
I
!

with possible semantic roles:
. Seed

| Continuity
! Commit

This supports the Blue Line lineage concept as an inspectable recurrence/rollback structure rather than hidden permanent model memory.
